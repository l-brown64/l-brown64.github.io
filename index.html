The cartoon was prepared by chatGPT. The utterance ".. BCD to seven channel EBCEDIC.. " was made by one of NYSDOT's computer staff at a meeting in 1973 or 1974. Clearly, it really stuck with me. EBCEDIC was a proprietary coding system then used by IBM and others. It was similar to ASCII which replaced it. I don't remember if the BCD/EBCEDIC conversion was the problem or a solution. BCD means binary coded decimal. The other pages of this site are two text documents and three mathematical tables. We hope the viewer finds the the papers interesting and the tables useful when converting between numbering systems of different bases: base 2, 8, 10, and 16. They are of my own design developed using the Libre Office spreadsheet program. As mentioned, the text documents include two papers. One describes the progression of relative computing power of various computers available over the years. It starts with mainframe models from the early sixties and progresses up to modern day laptops. The other paper discusses an alternative, easier way of coming up with negative numbers in the base two numbering system. The first paper was generated by chatGPT. The second is from content by multiple sources on the inerrant YouTube and authored by yours truly.
